CEE PREPARATION

Prepare smarter, not just harder.

Some lessons from the preparation journey. Tap each topic to explore.

A useful plan should fit your actual routine and leave room for revision.

  • Set small daily targets.
  • Prioritize difficult topics.
  • Include breaks and sleep.
  • Review your plan weekly.

Use questions to identify weak areas—not only to count your score.

  • Attempt questions without checking answers first.
  • Understand why each option is right or wrong.
  • Keep a short list of repeated mistakes.
  • Revisit incorrect questions later.

Regular revision helps keep earlier topics fresh while you learn new material.

  • Revise the same day when possible.
  • Use concise notes and diagrams.
  • Mix older topics into practice sessions.
  • Track topics that need another review.

Preparation is a long process. Protect your health and make your routine sustainable.

  • Get enough sleep.
  • Eat regular meals and drink water.
  • Take short movement breaks.
  • Ask for help when you feel overwhelmed.
