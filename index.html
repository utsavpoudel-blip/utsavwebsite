The Story Behind The White Coat

A childhood dream that never changed.

Utsav Poudel was born in Pokhara, Nepal, at what is now Pokhara Academy of Health Sciences, formerly Gandaki Hospital.

As a child, he was frequently unwell with cough, fever and recurring illnesses. His parents often took him to hospitals in Pokhara.

Watching doctors care for him and help him recover gradually shaped the way he saw medicine.

The dream of becoming a doctor stayed with him through changing schools, academic challenges and years of preparation.

“The dream remained the same. Only the distance between the dream and reality changed.”

CEE Preparation

Prepare Smarter.

What to study matters. But knowing how to study, revise and analyze your mistakes matters too.

Understand the syllabus first, then divide your preparation into learning, revision and testing.

  • Understand the complete syllabus.
  • Identify strong and weak areas.
  • Create realistic daily targets.
  • Keep regular revision cycles.
  • Use mock tests to measure progress.

Build strong concepts, revise repeatedly and practice MCQs after completing chapters.

Balance conceptual understanding, reactions, formulas and numerical problem solving.

Understand the meaning behind formulas and practice calculations regularly.

Use active recall, short revision notes and repeated revision of difficult topics.

Don't only check your score. Analyze every mistake, identify patterns and use them to improve.

Entrance preparation also includes understanding forms, notices, documents, deadlines and admission procedures.
